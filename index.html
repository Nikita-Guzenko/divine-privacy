Driver Applicant Privacy Notice

Divine Enterprises · Effective date: July 28, 2026

1. Who we are, and what this notice covers

Divine Enterprises is an interstate motor carrier authorized for hire, operating under USDOT number 1134917 and MC-461462, with offices at 3555 Cincinnati Ave., Rocklin, CA 95765.

This notice explains how we handle personal information from people who apply, or express interest in applying, to drive for us. It covers driver-applicant information only. It does not describe how we handle information about customers, vendors, or website visitors generally.

2. What we collect, and where it comes from

All of the information described here comes from you — from what you submit in a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead form, a job-board listing, or a driver application form we link to. We do not buy applicant lists.

Depending on which form you use, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your CDL class
  • Your years of CDL driving experience
  • Your endorsements (for example, Hazmat)
  • The earliest date you could start
  • Whether you are willing to run as part of a two-driver team
  • Your own description of your English proficiency
  • Any other information the specific form asks for, and anything else you choose to tell us

3. Why we use it

We use the information you submit to:

  • Contact you about the driver position you showed interest in;
  • Pre-screen you against the requirements of the role — in particular our team-driving requirement and the driver-qualification standards that apply to us under U.S. Department of Transportation and FMCSA rules;
  • Move you into a formal driver application if you want to continue, and keep the records that hiring and driver-qualification obligations require us to keep.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell applicant information, and we do not use it to target advertising to you or anyone else. We do share it with the service providers whose systems we actually use to run hiring:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) — when you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta hosts that form and holds your response until we retrieve it. Meta's own handling of that data is governed by its policies, not ours.
  • CareerPlug (divine-enterprises.careerplug.com) — our applicant-tracking system, where applications are received and managed.
  • IntelliApp / DriverAppOnline (intelliapp.driverapponline.com/c/divinetrans) — the driver-application platform used for the formal DOT driver application.
  • Google Workspace — used for our email and for record-keeping about applicants.

Mobile Information Sharing (SMS Privacy / 10DLC Mandatory Clause): No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to comply with DOT, FMCSA, or other regulatory obligations, or to establish or defend legal claims.

5. How long we keep it

We keep applicant information for as long as we need it to consider your application and to meet the record-keeping obligations that apply to us as a motor carrier and as an employer, and no longer than that. Because a driver applicant may re-apply or be considered for a later opening, we may retain your information after a particular decision is made for those purposes. You can ask us to delete it — see the next section.

6. California Notice at Collection for Job Applicants (CCPA/CPRA)

Divine Enterprises is a California employer. This section is our notice at collection to California residents who apply to drive for us, under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

Categories of personal information we collect, and why

Category Examples we actually collect Business purpose
Identifiers Name, phone number, email address To contact you about the driver position and to identify your application across our hiring systems
Professional or employment-related information CDL class, years of CDL experience, endorsements such as Hazmat, earliest start date, willingness to run as a team To evaluate whether you meet the requirements of the role and the driver-qualification standards that apply to us under DOT/FMCSA rules
Characteristics of protected classifications, where you volunteer them Your own statement of your English proficiency To assess a job-related requirement of interstate driving; we do not ask for, and do not want, protected-class information beyond what the role requires
Any other information you choose to provide Free-text notes or messages you send us To respond to you and to consider your application

Sale and sharing

We do not sell applicant personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the preceding 12 months. Applicant information is disclosed only to the service providers listed in section 4, for the business purposes described there, and where the law requires disclosure.

Retention

As stated in section 5: we keep applicant personal information for as long as it is needed to consider the application and to meet our record-keeping obligations as a motor carrier and employer, and no longer.

Your rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties it was disclosed to;
  • Delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to the exceptions the law allows — including information we are required to retain under DOT/FMCSA or other record-keeping rules;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you;
  • Not be retaliated against for exercising any of these rights. Exercising them will not affect how we consider your application.

How to exercise them: call us at (916) 781-7200, or call the recruiting line at (305) 413-8988, or write to us at the postal address below. Tell us which right you want to exercise. We will ask you for enough information to verify that you are the person whose information you are asking about — normally the name and phone number or email address you applied with — and we will use that information only to handle your request. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf; we may ask for proof of that authorization.
