Divine Enterprises Driver Recruiting SMS Program

Divine Enterprises operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with prospective CDL Class-A drivers regarding employment opportunities, application status, interview scheduling, and onboarding requirements.

How to Opt In

Prospective CDL Class-A drivers may opt in to receive recruiting SMS messages through either of the following direct channels:

Channel 1: Online Web Inquiry & Application Form

Prospective drivers can submit an inquiry directly through our online form below. Before submission, drivers must actively check the unchecked consent checkbox:

Thank you. Your inquiry has been received. Our recruiting team will follow up shortly.

Note: Meta Lead Forms (Facebook & Instagram Ads) utilize the exact same unchecked consent disclosure prior to lead submission.

Channel 2: Direct Keyword Opt-in (Text-In)

Drivers may also enroll directly by texting START or SUBSCRIBE to (916) 720-0062 from their mobile device after reviewing our program terms.

Terms, Rates & Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your application activity and engagement with our recruiting team. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS enrollment is completely optional. You can apply for any position without enrolling in text messaging via our online driver application or by calling our office directly at (916) 720-0062.

Opt-Out & Support

Reply STOP to cancel: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from Divine Enterprises. You will receive one final confirmation message confirming you are unsubscribed.

Reply HELP for help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or call us directly at (916) 720-0062.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
