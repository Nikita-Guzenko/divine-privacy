Divine Enterprises Driver Recruiting SMS Program

Divine Enterprises operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with prospective CDL Class-A drivers regarding employment opportunities, application status, interview scheduling, and onboarding requirements.

How to Opt In

Drivers may consent to receive recruiting text messages through either of the following methods:

  • 1. Online Job Inquiries & Lead Forms (e.g., Facebook Lead Ads):
    When inquiring about open driving positions through our online recruitment forms, drivers enter their contact information and are presented with the following explicit disclaimer before submitting:
    "By submitting this form, you authorize Divine Enterprises to send recruiting and employment-related SMS text messages and phone calls to the phone number provided above. Consent is not a condition of employment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or reply HELP for assistance. View our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms at https://divine-privacy.vercel.app/sms-privacy."
  • 2. Direct Inbound Keyword (Text-In):
    You may also enroll directly by texting START or SUBSCRIBE to (916) 720-0062 from your mobile device.

Terms, Rates & Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your application activity and engagement with our recruiting team. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS enrollment is completely optional. You can apply for any position without enrolling in text messaging via our online driver application or by calling our office directly at (916) 781-7200 ext 239.

Opt-Out & Support

Reply STOP to cancel: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from Divine Enterprises. You will receive one final confirmation message confirming you are unsubscribed.

Reply HELP for help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or call us directly at (916) 781-7200 ext 239.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
