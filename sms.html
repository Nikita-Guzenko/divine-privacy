Divine Enterprises Driver Recruiting SMS Program

Divine Enterprises operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with prospective CDL Class-A drivers regarding employment opportunities, application status, interview scheduling, and onboarding requirements.

How to Opt In

SMS enrollment is optional and separate from a job application. You can apply for a position without enrolling in SMS.

After reviewing this page, text START or SUBSCRIBE to (916) 720-0062 from the mobile number where you want messages.

Text START to enroll

If you enroll by keyword, you will receive this confirmation:

Divine Enterprises: You are subscribed to recurring driver recruiting texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Terms: https://divine-privacy.vercel.app/sms-terms Privacy: https://divine-privacy.vercel.app/sms-privacy

Terms, Rates & Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your application activity and engagement with our recruiting team. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS enrollment is optional and is not a condition of employment. You can apply for any position without enrolling in text messaging via our online driver application or by calling our office directly at (916) 720-0062.

Opt-Out & Support

Reply STOP to cancel: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from Divine Enterprises. You will receive one final confirmation message confirming you are unsubscribed.

Reply HELP for help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or call us directly at (916) 720-0062.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
