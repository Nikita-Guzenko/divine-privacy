Driver Recruiting SMS Terms

Divine Enterprises · Effective September 26, 2026

Program

Divine Enterprises sends recurring, two-way text messages to drivers who enroll in its Driver Recruiting SMS program. Messages concern Divine Enterprises CDL-A driver positions, qualifications, the driver application, and application follow-up. This program does not enroll you in third-party offers or freight-customer notifications.

Enrollment and cost

After reading the SMS enrollment page, text START or SUBSCRIBE to (916) 720-0062 from the mobile number at which you want messages. Enrollment is optional and is not required to apply for a position. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Stop messages or get help

Reply STOP to opt out of further recruiting texts. Reply HELP for help. You can also call Divine Enterprises recruiting at (916) 781-7200 ext 239. After opting out, text START if you want to enroll again.

Delivery and privacy

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages. We handle SMS information under our Driver Recruiting SMS Privacy Notice. Our general Driver Recruitment Terms of Service also apply to the use of our recruiting website and forms; accepting those terms alone does not enroll you in SMS.
