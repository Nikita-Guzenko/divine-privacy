Driver Recruiting SMS Privacy Notice

Divine Enterprises · Effective September 26, 2026

This notice covers the Divine Enterprises Driver Recruiting SMS program. Our broader Driver Applicant Privacy Notice covers applications and recruiting forms.

Information and use

When you text us, we receive your mobile number, message content, and message time. We record whether you opted in or out, the keyword source, and the time of that choice. We use this information to answer questions about Divine Enterprises driver positions, discuss qualifications, provide the driver application link, follow up on an application, and honor your messaging preferences. We do not use purchased applicant lists for this program.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell or share your mobile number, SMS opt-in data, or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging and record-keeping providers process this information strictly on our behalf to operate the recruiting program. We may disclose information when required by law.

Your choices

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance. After opting out, text START to enroll again. SMS is optional; you can apply without it.
